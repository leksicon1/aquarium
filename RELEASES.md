## 6.2.0

- Feed the fish: press F (or pick another key under Scene → Fish) and food drops in from above the water. The live wallpaper has "Feed the fish" in its menu by the clock.
- Web and email addresses in the developer's note can be clicked.
- Camera movement is continuous.
- The water is always fully clear.
- The screensaver draws only on the screens you picked.
- The app checks for new versions and can update itself.
