## 7.25.0

1. An octopus lives on the reef: a den in the rocks, crawling on eight arms, short jets, and colour that matches where it is.
2. Mouths that work, modelled from each fish's own shape: they open and close as fish breathe, gape and bite.
3. Real eyes that look around and follow food, passing fish, and you.
4. Fluttering front (pectoral) fins on every fish.
5. A rebuilt sea turtle: a sculpted shell with its plates, a proper neck, a beak that opens, new eyes.
6. A rebuilt shark: teeth, a third eyelid, wing-like side fins, a real swimming motion; it comes up to the surface on about half its visits.
7. A blue-spotted stingray that glides over the sand and settles.
8. Manta wing tips that bend and curl.
9. A slower whale that cruises just under the surface, clear of the reef.
10. Visitors swim in out of the blue distance and away into it.
11. Better feeding: natural-coloured food that sinks, glints and crumbles when bitten.
12. Starfish creeping across the sand.
13. Feather-duster worms that snap shut when a fish comes close.
14. More corals (mushroom corals, feather dusters, two new anemones); bushes and branching corals are now fully 3D.
15. Corals breathe, and grass and soft corals bend aside as fish brush past.
16. A cleaning station where small wrasse groom bigger fish.
17. New camera modes: follow the octopus, follow one fish after another, a slow tour of the caves and arches, or a mix.
18. Rocks sit in the sand, with drifts of coarse shell sand, and look better on the lowest quality levels.
19. Wide view: a new setting that shows the whole scene across two or three monitors.
20. Smoother on laptops: high-refresh screens are no longer held to 60 fps, and the graphics card can be picked by name.

Older versions stay available on the website and here. Updates are never forced: the app offers them and you choose.

## 7.0.3

- The app is now called Ultra Aquarium (it was Reef Aquarium).
- A proper installer: Start menu shortcut, an entry in Installed apps, and the app opens when it finishes. About 12.6 MB.
- Updates download the new installer, verify it and run it.
- The screensaver runs from the same folder as the app.
- Watch full screen covers the screens you picked, and the settings preview pauses while it runs.
- Every regular fish is larger, from 10% for the smallest species up to 45% for the largest.
- Feed the fish with F (the key can be changed); the live wallpaper has it in the menu by the clock.
- The settings window fits any screen and scaling, with one window at a time.
